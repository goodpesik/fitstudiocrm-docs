# 7.4 Client Card

> The **Client card** tab shows the QR code of the client card. The code is scanned at the studio front desk to find the client profile and for [automatic registration by card](/en/pages/processing#_610-automatic-registration-on-card-scan).

The **«Your client card»** screen contains:

- the studio name and the **«CLIENT»** badge;
- the QR code with the card number on a white background;
- the card number under the QR code;
- the client name and the caption «Show at the front desk».

If no card has been issued to the client, «No card yet. Ask at the studio's front desk.» is shown. The card is issued by the administrator in [Clients](/en/menu/clients).

> ℹ️ The tab is available only for the **Customer** role.

---

## Check-in by the Studio QR Code

Separately from the client card, the studio can show its own QR code at the front desk (a banner on a tablet). The client scans it with the phone camera, and the app opens the **«Check in to a class»** screen for self check-in to today's workout. If several clients of the studio are linked to the account, the app asks to choose the client («Who is checking in?»). Rules and messages are in [Self check-in by QR code](/en/pages/processing#_611-self-check-in-by-qr-code).

If the user is not logged in, the screen shows «Sign in to the app to check in to a class with the studio's QR code.» and the **«Sign in»** button; after login the check-in continues.

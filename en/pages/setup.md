# 4. Initial Setup

> The order of the initial system setup

---

## 4.1 Add and Configure Administrators

> Invite administrators and managers by email on the [Users](/en/login/users) tab with a role. Access is granted after the invited person first signs in through Google with the same email.  
> Then set for each user the phone, the hourly rate and, for the **Адмін** (Admin) role, the **Нараховувати бонуси** (Accrue bonuses) switch. Only the **Супер Адмін** (Super Admin) can change roles.

---

## 4.2 Add Categories

> Add and configure categories.  
> Categories can also be added or edited later.  
> It is recommended to first create the base categories that will be used when creating workouts.

---

## 4.3 Add Plans

> Add the base plans.  
> Plans can also be created later, and existing ones can be edited.

---

## 4.4 Add Rooms

> Create and configure rooms.  
> The room color is used in the calendar if a workout has no coach (rental) or the coach has no color selected.

---

## 4.5 Add Coaches

> Add and configure coaches.  
> They can also be added and edited later.

---

## 4.6 Add Workouts

> Add and configure workouts.

---

## 4.7 Add Clients

> Add clients.

### 4.7.1 Configuring Client Plans

> For clients who, at the time of the switch, have active plans from another system, create plans with the number of remaining workouts matching the actual balance.  
> Plans paid before the switch are sold to clients at a price of **0**.  
> After the sale, these plans can be deleted from the plan list.  
> Book the clients into the workouts they were previously registered for.

---

### 4.7.2 Importing Clients from an Excel File

> Import is done on the **Клієнти** (Clients) page with the **Завантажити клієнтів з Excel** (Upload clients from Excel) button. File format, import steps and duplicate handling — in [Excel import and export of clients](/en/menu/clients#_3214-excel-import-and-export-of-clients).

---

## 4.8 Getting Ready to Operate

> On the [Settings → Mobile Application](/en/login/settings#mobile-application) tab, turn on **Використовувати мобільний додаток для клієнтів** (Use the mobile app for clients).
> In the **Посилання на застосунок і повідомлення** (App link and messages) section, download the QR code (**Завантажити QR код**) or the banner (**Завантажити баннер**) and print it.
> It is recommended to place the QR code in the studio: at the reception, in the changing rooms. By scanning the code, a client installs the app, books workouts, views their plans and receives notifications.
> Fill in the [Studio Information](/en/login/settings#studio-information) tab: clients see the studio name, logo, description, photos and contacts in the app.

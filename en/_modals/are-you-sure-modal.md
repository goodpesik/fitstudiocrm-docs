> The "Are you sure you want to continue?" modal window

> Clicking the "Cancel" button or the close button in the top-right corner (an X icon). Changes are not saved. The modal window closes.

> Clicking the "Continue" button performs the previously triggered action (change, save, delete, etc.). The modal window closes.

# 3.6 Memberships

![](../../_media/plan-1.png ':size=300x181')

## 3.6.1 Membership card

> A membership card contains:

- **Name**
- **Membership term, months** ("Строк абонемента міc.:")
- **Number of workouts** ("Кількість тренувань:")
- **Price** ("Ціна:", UAH)
- **Buttons** (SuperAdmin and Admin roles): **Delete** (requires confirmation; afterwards "Абонемент успішно видалено" (membership deleted) is shown) and **Edit** (the **Edit membership** ("Редагувати абонемент") window).

The card list is paginated (10, 20 or 50 cards per page).

---

## 3.6.2 Add a membership

> The "+" button above the list of cards (SuperAdmin and Admin roles) opens the **Add membership** ("Додати абонемент") modal window:

![](../../_media/plan-2.png ':size=240x240')

**Contains:**

- **Membership type** ("Тип абонементу") — required: **Базовий** (basic, group workouts), **Персональний** (personal), **Оренда** (rent).
- **Name** ("Назва") — required.
- **Membership term, months** ("Строк абонемента міс.") — the number of months, at most 100. For a single-session membership the value is 0.
- **Number of workouts** ("Кількість тренувань") — a number, at most 100.
- **Price** ("Ціна") — a number.

Buttons: **Close** ("Закрити"), **Save** ("Зберегти").

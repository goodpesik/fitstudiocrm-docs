# 7.9 Ratings and Reviews

> Clients rate classes they've attended right in the app. These ratings make up the studio's rating and each coach's rating.

---

## How a Client Leaves a Rating

After an attended class, the home screen offers to rate the last class. This offer is shown only to a client whose class is marked **Visited**, and only if the studio has not disabled the **"Offer to Rate a Class"** ("Пропонувати оцінити заняття") toggle on the [Settings → Mobile App](/en/login/settings#mobile-application) tab.

The rating is from one to five stars, with an optional comment. The client can decline: the prompt then disappears and is not shown again for that class.

---

## When the Rating Becomes Visible

Stars appear publicly only after **five ratings**. Before that, clients cannot see the rating at all: an average of one or two ratings means nothing and could unfairly hurt the studio or coach.

The public list of reviews shows **positive** reviews — rated four and above, up to ten of the most recent. Critical reviews do not appear in this list, but **all ratings affect the average**.

---

## Who Sees What

| Role | What's available |
|---|---|
| **Client** | The studio's rating and reviews about it — by tapping the star in the studio header on Home |
| **Coach** | The **Ratings** tab: their own average rating and clients' reviews about them |
| **Administrator** | The **Ratings** tab: all of the studio's coaches with their ratings, with a link through to each one's reviews |

> ℹ️ A coach sees only reviews about themselves — reviews about colleagues are not available to them. A client does not see the **Ratings** tab at all.

In the list of coaches, those who do not yet have enough ratings are shown with a dash instead of a number: this shows the coach is active, while it's still too early to judge their rating.

---

## Ratings in the Admin Panel

The same information from the studio's side — in [Reports → Rating](/en/menu/reports#_3-8-6-rating): the studio's overall rating, each coach's rating, and the list of reviews. The **Super Admin** and **Admin** roles can delete a review.

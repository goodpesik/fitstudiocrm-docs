# 7.4 Client Card

> The **Client Card** tab shows the **QR code** for your client card. It is scanned at the studio reception to quickly find your profile.

<!-- TODO: screenshot of the screen with the card's QR code -->

- Title **"Your Client Card"** ("Ваша картка клієнта").
- A large QR code containing your card number.
- The **card number** printed below the QR code.

> ℹ️ This tab is available **only for the Client role**. Coaches and administrators do not see it.

> If a card number is not yet linked to the client — the screen is empty. In that case, contact the studio administrator so they can issue/link a card in the [Clients](/en/menu/clients) section.

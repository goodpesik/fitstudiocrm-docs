# 3.4 Coaches

![](../../_media/coaches-1.png ':size=200x70')

## 3.4.1 Coach card

> A coach card contains:

- **Name**
- **Avatar** (if uploaded; otherwise a default one is used)
- **Phone**
- **Buttons**:
  - **Delete** — cannot delete a coach who has scheduled sessions
  - **Edit**

---

## 3.4.2 Add a coach

> The **Add coach** button is above the list of cards, marked with a "+" icon.

> Clicking it opens a modal window:

![](../../_media/coach-2.png ':size=150x90')

**Contains:**

- Avatar upload
- Text field **Name** (required)
- **Phone** field (required, numeric format)
- **Calendar color** (required field) — chosen from a palette of light shades,
  a 7×5 grid. There is no free color picker any more: on light shades the text
  on an event card stays readable whatever the color. A coach saved before the
  palette keeps their color and is drawn with the nearest allowed shade
- **Birthday** — date picker, optional.

> ℹ️ **Coach payroll calculation:**
> All payroll parameters (rate, calculation type `PerPerson` / `PerWorkout`) are defined **on the session category** ([Settings → Categories](/en/login/settings#categories)), not on the coach card. The coach card has neither category selection nor a rate override.
>
> More detail: [Information on processing visits, membership session deductions, and reminders](/en/pages/processing)

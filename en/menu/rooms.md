# 3.5 Rooms

![](../../_media/rooms-1.png ':size=300x181')

## 3.5.1 Room card

> A room card contains:

- **Name**
- **Room color**
- **Capacity**
- **Buttons**:
  - **Delete** — cannot delete a room that has scheduled sessions
  - **Edit**

---

## 3.5.2 Add a room

> The **Add room** button is above the list of cards, marked with a "+" icon.

> Clicking it opens a modal window:

![](../../_media/room-2.png ':size=200x217')

**Contains:**

- Text field **Name**
- Numeric field **Maximum number of people**
- A color picker to choose the room's color

> The «Увага!» (Attention!) modal window with the text «Ви впевнені що хочете продовжити?» (Are you sure you want to continue?)

> The **Відміна** (Cancel) button or the cross in the top-right corner closes the window; the action is not performed and changes are not saved.

> The **Продовжити** (Continue) button performs the action the window was opened for (change, save, delete, etc.) and closes the window.

# 3.6 Memberships

![](../../_media/plan-1.png ':size=300x181')

## 3.6.1 Membership card

> A membership card contains:

- **Name**
- **Membership term (months)**
- **Number of sessions**
- **Price**
- **Buttons**: *Delete* and *Edit*

---

## 3.6.2 Add a membership

> The **Add membership** button is above the list of cards, marked with a "+" icon.

> Clicking it opens a modal window:

![](../../_media/plan-2.png ':size=240x240')

**Contains:**

- **Membership type** (Regular, Personal, Rental)
- Text field **Name**
- **Membership term, months** (numeric field, in months). For a single-visit membership, the value = 0
- **Number of sessions**
- **Price**
